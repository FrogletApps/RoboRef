## 3. Requirements for Forks & Modified Versions

If you fork, modify, build, or redistribute a modified version of RoboRef (whether client apps or sync servers), you **must** do all of the following before distributing binaries or hosting a public web/PWA instance:

1. **Rename the Application:** Do not call your modified app or hosted service "RoboRef" (or include "RoboRef" in its title, e.g., *"RoboRef Pro"* or *"RECF RoboRef"*). Choose a clearly distinct name.
2. **Replace Brand Artwork & Icons:** Remove and replace all RoboRef logos, splash/favicon artwork, and app icons (`app/assets/icons/` and derived Android/iOS/Web platform icons).
3. **Change Package / Bundle Identifiers:** Change the application ID / bundle identifier (`app.roboref`) so builds cannot collide with or impersonate official RoboRef releases on devices or app stores.
4. **No Implied Endorsement:** Do not use the RoboRef Marks, `roboref.app`, or similar domains/handles in any way that suggests your fork, service, or event deployment is endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially affiliated with RoboRef or FrogletApps.
